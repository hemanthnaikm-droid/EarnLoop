EARNLOOP · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Publisher action required: This is a starter policy based on the currently described EarnLoop features. Before public release, replace any incomplete details with your real operator/contact information and verify it against every Firebase collection, log, analytics tool, and third-party service actually used by your deployment. Do not publish this page as a claim that an unverified practice is already in place.

1. Information EarnLoop may process

Depending on the features you use, EarnLoop may process account identifiers such as email address and Firebase user ID; task listings and proposed rewards; applications; work submissions and evidence; reports and dispute records; account-deletion requests; and technical security logs. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, or other sensitive information in task descriptions or evidence unless a feature explicitly requires it and suitable safeguards have been implemented.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, display and manage tasks, process applications and submissions, handle reports and disputes, prevent abuse, respond to account requests, and maintain service reliability. Any additional analytics, advertising, or payment processing must be disclosed here before it is enabled.

3. Task visibility and sharing

Task content and related records may be visible to the customer, applicants, or other authorized users according to the feature and access rules. Do not include private contact details or confidential information in public task content. The operator must verify the live Firestore rules and frontend queries before release.

4. Service providers

EarnLoop uses Firebase services for authentication and database functionality and may be hosted through Vercel. Those providers process data according to their own terms and privacy documentation. Additional providers must be listed here before being enabled.

5. Retention, disputes, and deletion requests

You may request account deletion through the account menu when the feature is available. A request may require re-authentication and may be held for review if a dispute or legal/operational retention obligation exists. Submitting a request does not mean deletion is immediate. The operator must apply and document a retention schedule, remove or de-identify eligible records, and explain any records retained and why.

6. Security

EarnLoop is intended to use access controls and server-side validation to protect records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The operator must configure and test Firestore rules, App Check, backups, monitoring, rate limits, and incident response before representing those controls as active.

7. Children and eligibility

EarnLoop's minimum age and eligibility requirements must be set by the operator before launch and displayed during registration. Do not use the service if you do not meet those requirements.

8. Your choices and contact

You can manage account access through Firebase Authentication and request account deletion through the available account settings. For privacy questions or requests, contact the service operator using the official contact details published by EarnLoop.

Before publishing: Add a real support/privacy contact, confirm the legal entity/operator, minimum age, exact data retention periods, complaint/grievance process, applicable jurisdictional disclosures, and any enabled analytics or payment providers. Remove this notice only after the policy has been reviewed and finalized.
